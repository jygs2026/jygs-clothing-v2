Short runs, on purpose

Four hundred pieces per volume. When a pattern sells through we retire it rather than re-cut it thinner. The waitlist exists so we make what is wanted and not a warehouse of what isn't.

Cloth we can name

Japanese cotton twill, Italian wool melton, loopback from a Porto mill that has been at it since 1954. Every piece lists its mill, its weight and how it will move in the third year.

Made to order, mended free

Tailoring and outerwear are cut to your measurements in six weeks. Bring anything of ours back for repair, for as long as you own it — we would rather mend than sell you a second one.
